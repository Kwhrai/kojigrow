From the Editor

Japanese Roots. West Coast Curiosity.

A note on why KOJIGROW exists — and what we want to bring to the table.

ai.
Ai Kawahara

Editor · ORYZAE / CTO

November 18, 2026·2 min read

Pull up a chair.

A jar of miso. A cold drink. The small decision about what sounds good after a long day.

Those are the kinds of starting points we want for KOJIGROW. Everyday things with a little more going on beneath the surface.

A little about the person behind the edit.

I’m Ai Kawahara, CTO at ORYZAE, a fermentation researcher, and a registered dietitian. My work is rooted in Japanese fermentation. This publication is a place to open that conversation beyond the research and product-development setting.

There is plenty to be curious about: how a food gets its flavor, what a familiar ingredient can become, and why a drink fits one moment better than another.

We want to make those questions inviting enough to follow, even if you have never heard the word koji before.

Start with the food.
Stay for the backstory.

Why the West Coast?

LA gives this publication a point of curiosity: the relationship between food, drinks, and the rhythms of everyday life. A walk, a class, a market stop, a glass shared with a friend. What might Japanese fermentation bring to those moments?

This is an editorial lens, not a claim that our team is based in LA. When we publish field notes, we will make clear what we experienced firsthand, who we spoke with, and what is still a question.

What you will find here.

Koji, What? starts with the basics. Sip Check explores drinks and ways to enjoy them. The Mix makes room for food and everyday discoveries. LA Field Notes will follow the people, places, and observations we can report on directly.

Some pieces will include a little science. Others will simply give you something new to try. Throughout, we will distinguish research from opinion, and a serving suggestion from a tested health benefit.

And who is publishing it?

KOJIGROW is published by ORYZAE, the team behind KOJIPOP. That connection belongs in the open. When we write about our own products or work, we will identify them as ours.

For now, welcome to the first edit. Start wherever your curiosity takes you.

Ai Kawahara
Editor, KOJIGROW · CTO, ORYZAE
